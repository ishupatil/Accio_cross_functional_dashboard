## 3. Dealership Commission & Payout Efficiency

We evaluated each showroom's sales performance and compared it to the commissions we paid them. This reveals which stores are cost-effective versus which ones are eroding our profit margins.

### Key Insight:
* **The Efficiency Mismatch:** **Ahuja Motors Suryapet** generated **$89.1M** in revenue and only charged us **$13.7M** in commissions (a very efficient 15.38% payout ratio).
* Conversely, **Dada Motors Dhanbad** generated similar revenue (**$94.0M**) but charged us **$31.0M** in commissions (a massive 33.01% payout ratio). Dada Motors is costing us double the commission rate!

## 4. CFO Actionable Recommendations

1. **Renegotiate Outlier Commission Structures:** Review the contracts for dealers like Dada Motors (33.01% payout ratio) and Nagi Motors (29.04% payout ratio). Standardize commission payout caps closer to the network average of **20%** to save millions.
2. **Double Down on High-Efficiency Showrooms:** Allocate higher vehicle inventory batches to showrooms like Ahuja Motors (Suryapet) which operate at high sales velocities with low commission overheads.
3. **Establish a Margin Safety Floor:** Given that our Net Profit Margin has fluctuated from **59% down to 38%** as logistics and manufacturing costs rose in mid-2023, the executive board should set a **30% margin safety floor** to prevent operating at a loss.
