# Mathematical Calculations & KPI Documentation

This document compiles the exact mathematical formulas, PostgreSQL database sources, and Pandas processing logic used to calculate all KPIs and chart datasets across your dashboard.

---

## 📈 1. Core Financial KPIs (Home Page & Simulator)

### A. Gross Revenue (\(R_{\text{gross}}\))
* **Business Definition:** The total corporate invoicing revenue generated from vehicle sales, spares, and service income.
* **Mathematical Formula:**
  \[R_{\text{gross}} = R_{\text{vehicle}} + R_{\text{spares}} + R_{\text{service}}\]
  \[R_{\text{vehicle}} = \sum \text{net\_amount} \quad (\text{from } \text{sales\_transaction.orders})\]
  \[R_{\text{spares}} = \sum \text{credit\_amount} \quad (\text{GL account 4100})\]
  \[R_{\text{service}} = \sum \text{credit\_amount} \quad (\text{GL account 4200})\]

### B. Operating Expenses (\(E_{\text{opex}}\))
* **Business Definition:** The total cash outflows required to run operations, including COGS, manufacturing, payroll, marketing, logistics, commissions, and administration.
* **Mathematical Formula:**
  \[E_{\text{opex}} = \sum_{j=1}^{m} \text{debit\_amount}_j \quad \text{where } \text{account\_type} = \text{'Expense'}\]
* **PostgreSQL Source:**
  * Tables: `finance_accounting.ledger_entries` merged with `finance_accounting.gl_accounts` on `account_id`
  * Columns: `debit_amount` (when `account_type = 'Expense'`), filtered by account codes:
    * `5000`: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
    * `5100`: Manufacturing Costs
    * `5200`: Salaries & Wages
    * `5300`: Marketing Expenses
    * `5400`: Logistics Expenses
    * `5500`: Dealer Commissions
    * `5600`: Administrative Expenses
* **Pandas Implementation:**
  ```python
  expenses_df = merged_ledger[merged_ledger["account_type"] == "Expense"]
  total_expenses = expenses_df["debit_amount"].sum()
  ```

### C. Corporate Net Earnings (\(P_{\text{net}}\))
* **Business Definition:** The actual net operating profit of the company.
* **Mathematical Formula:**
  \[P_{\text{net}} = R_{\text{gross}} - E_{\text{opex}}\]

### D. Operating Net Margin (\(M_{\text{net}}\))
* **Business Definition:** The percentage of gross sales that represents net profit.
* **Mathematical Formula:**
  \[M_{\text{net}} = \left( \frac{P_{\text{net}}}{R_{\text{gross}}} \right) \times 100\]
* **Pandas Implementation:**
  ```python
  net_profit_margin = (net_profit / total_revenue) * 100.0
  ```

## 🏬 2. Showroom & Commission KPIs (Showrooms Page)

### A. Showroom Payout Ratio (\(PR_d\))
* **Business Definition:** The percentage of revenue generated by a showroom that is paid back as dealer commission.
* **Mathematical Formula for Dealer \(d\):**
  \[PR_d = \left( \frac{\sum \text{payout\_amount}_d}{\sum \text{net\_amount}_d} \right) \times 100\]
* **PostgreSQL Source:**
  * Payouts: `finance_accounting.dealer_payouts.payout_amount`
  * Sales: `sales_transaction.orders.net_amount`
* **Pandas Implementation:**
  ```python
  perf["payout_ratio_pct"] = (perf["total_commission"] / perf["total_revenue"]) * 100.0
  ```

### B. Critical Commission Outliers
* **Business Definition:** Showrooms whose payout ratio exceeds the corporate target threshold of 20%.
* **Mathematical Formula:**
  \[\text{Outliers Count} = \sum_{d \in D} \mathbb{I}(PR_d > 20.0)\]
  *(where \(\mathbb{I}\) is an indicator function returning 1 if the condition is met, and 0 otherwise)*

## 👥 3. Customer & Segment KPIs (Customer Page)

### A. Average Order Value (AOV)
* **Business Definition:** The average revenue generated per transaction.
* **Mathematical Formula:**
  \[\text{AOV} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{net\_amount}_i}{n} = \text{Mean of } \text{net\_amount}\]
* **Pandas Implementation:**
  ```python
  aov_mean = orders_df["net_amount"].mean()
  ```

## 🎛️ 4. CEO Scenario Simulator Projections (Simulator Page)

The Scenario Simulator performs dynamic browser-based calculations based on sliders.

### A. Simulated Commissions Payout (\(C_{\text{sim}}\))
* **Formula:**
  If a cap \(C\) (e.g., 20%) is applied, any dealer's payout ratio exceeding the cap is restricted to the cap:
  \[PR_d^{\text{sim}} = \min(PR_d, C)\]
  \[C_{\text{sim}} = \sum_{d \in D} \left( \text{Revenue}_d \times \frac{PR_d^{\text{sim}}}{100} \right)\]
* **Simulated Commission Savings (\(\Delta C\)):**
  \[\Delta C = C_{\text{baseline}} - C_{\text{sim}}\]

### B. Simulated Net Profit (\(P_{\text{sim}}\))
* **Formula:**
  Adjusts baseline opex by adding marketing and logistics swings, and subtracting commission savings:
  \[P_{\text{sim}} = R_{\text{gross}} - \left( E_{\text{opex}} - \Delta C + \Delta \text{Logistics} + \Delta \text{Marketing} \right)\]
